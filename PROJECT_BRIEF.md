# Loyalty Social Ultra Lounge Website Project Brief

## Project Name

Loyalty Social Ultra Lounge

## Website Domain

loyaltysocialmv.com

The public-facing brand name on the website should remain **Loyalty Social Ultra Lounge**. Do not overuse "loyaltysocialmv" on the visible website; that name is mainly for the domain and URL.

## Business Overview

Loyalty Social Ultra Lounge is an upscale social lounge and nightlife destination serving Baltimore County, Baltimore, Randallstown, and the surrounding Maryland market.

The venue offers:

- Food
- Drinks
- Weekly events
- Professional mixers
- Private nightly events
- VIP table reservations
- Private event experiences
- Social club atmosphere

This is not a hookah lounge and not a cigar lounge. Do not promote hookah, cigars, or smoking services anywhere on the website.

## Target Audience

Primary audience:

- Young professionals
- Creatives
- Entrepreneurs
- Nightlife crowd
- Social influencers
- 21+ guests
- Strong focus on 25+ mature nightlife
- Professionals looking for mixers, upscale events, food, drinks, and VIP experiences

The tone should attract a stylish, grown, social crowd without feeling cheap, overly loud, or generic.

Audience experience keywords:

- Grown
- Sexy
- Upscale
- Exclusive
- Professional
- Cultural
- Nightlife
- Social
- VIP
- Elevated

## Brand Feel

The website should feel like walking into a private luxury lounge.

Visual mood:

- Ultra luxury
- Black and charcoal gray background
- Egyptian gold accents
- Modern regal design
- Shimmering gold highlights
- Clean luxury
- Crisp imagery
- Elegant spacing
- High-end lounge atmosphere
- Soft shadows
- Premium lighting
- Black marble and gold marble accents
- Wall-panel inspired sections
- VIP invitation energy

Avoid a cheap club flyer look on the main website. Event flyers can remain bold, but the website itself should feel elevated and controlled.

## Color Palette

Primary colors:

- Matte Black: `#050505`
- Soft Black: `#0B0B0B`
- Charcoal Gray: `#1A1A1A`
- Deep Graphite: `#242424`
- Egyptian Gold: `#C9A24A`
- Bright Gold Accent: `#F4D06F`
- Antique Gold: `#9F7A28`
- Warm White: `#F5F0E6`

Suggested gradient:

```css
background: linear-gradient(135deg, #050505 0%, #111111 45%, #242424 100%);
```

## Implementation Notes

- Use the brand name "Loyalty Social Ultra Lounge" throughout the public site.
- Position the lounge as upscale, cultural, mature, and VIP-focused.
- Lead with reservations, private events, weekly events, food, drinks, and social atmosphere.
- Keep the visual language controlled, premium, black/gold, and photo-forward.
- Exclude hookah, cigar, and smoking references from all copy and imagery choices.
